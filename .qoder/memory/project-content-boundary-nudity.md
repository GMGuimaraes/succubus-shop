The game's sensual content stays at suggestive tone (art, poses, flirting dialogue) behind
the `adult_content_confirmed` toggle. There is **no explicit nudity**, and a request to
"desativar a censura de nudez" was declined with the boundary stated to the user. The
toggle is agreed to change only voice and art, never progression: economy, servos,
moments, achievements and every door must measure identical in both modes.

**Why:** the user chose this line deliberately as the design ceiling for the project, and
it is a standing constraint, not a one-off refusal. The A/B-equality rule exists so the
toggle can never become a hidden progression penalty.

**How to apply:** do not propose explicit sexual content, and do not treat "make the
sensual scenes stronger" as license to cross into nudity — stay inside art, pose, framing
and wording. When touching the filter, keep the probe proving that the two modes' numbers
match exactly. Related: [[project-succubus-shop-repo]],
[[probes-are-assets-not-junk]].
